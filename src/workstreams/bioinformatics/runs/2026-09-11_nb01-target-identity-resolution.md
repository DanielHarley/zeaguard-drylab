## Interpretation

`RESOLVED_HIGH_CONFIDENCE` is a computational classification under the proposed policy. `AMBIGUOUS` means the current evidence does not sufficiently distinguish competitive candidates. `UNRESOLVED` means the declared workflow did not produce sufficient evidence for a unique association. Failure of a threshold does not demonstrate an incorrect identity.

## What this run does NOT establish

This run does not establish experimentally confirmed target identity, biological function, gene presence or absence, transcript completeness, assembly correctness, scientific validity of the thresholds, or readiness for candidate design. Specifically, the `UNRESOLVED` result for `dsRNase-3` does not demonstrate that the gene is absent.
