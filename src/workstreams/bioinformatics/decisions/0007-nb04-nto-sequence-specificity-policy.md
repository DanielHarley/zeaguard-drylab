## Interpretation and governance

B5 R05 governs individualized concern documentation. R16 governs qualified
negative dispositions within assessed scope. Homology, triggers, concern,
absence and biological effect remain distinct. An attributed correspondence can
remain DETECTED_HOMOLOGY_WITHOUT_RESOLVED_BIOLOGICAL_IMPLICATION. Scientific
insufficiency can remain UNRESOLVED after completed valid human review.
Missing gate review leaves certification pending.

B6 A1 requires a qualified external human, distinct from the producer, for G0
certification. No staffing matrix or advance reviewer assignment is required for
this CP0 work. Computational tests do not supply that independent review.
